Best Time to Visit Antalya

Antalya has a long season. This guide shows typical air and sea temperatures and which months suit which kind of holiday.

Which month for which holiday?

  • Swimming and beach life: late May to early October. June and September give a good balance of warm sea and fewer crowds.
  • Sightseeing and ruins: April, May, late September and October are more comfortable than the peak summer heat.
  • Golf (Belek): courses are busiest from October to April; summer is hot for a round.
  • Lowest prices: November to March, with cooler weather, more rain and a quiet beach scene.

What to pack

  • High-factor sunscreen, hat and sunglasses from May to October.
  • A light jacket for evenings in spring and autumn, and rain gear in winter.
  • Comfortable shoes for stone streets and ancient sites.
  • Modest clothing (covered shoulders and knees) if you plan to visit mosques.

Frequently asked questions

When is the sea warmest in Antalya?

July and August, around 27–28 °C.

Is October a good month for Antalya?

Yes. Air temperatures are roughly 17–27 °C and the sea is still around 23–24 °C, with fewer crowds than summer.

Is Antalya worth visiting in winter?

Yes for sightseeing, golf and low prices, but it is not a swimming holiday: the sea is about 16–18 °C and rain is more likely.
