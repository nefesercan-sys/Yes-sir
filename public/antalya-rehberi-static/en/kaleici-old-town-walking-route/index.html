Kaleiçi Walking Route

A relaxed half-day route through Antalya's historic old town, from the Roman gate to the harbour.

Updated: 2026-10-24

The route in order

  • Hadrian's Gate: a triple-arched gate built to honour a visit by the Roman emperor Hadrian, and the most picturesque entrance to the old town.
  • Yivli Minaret: the landmark mosque with a tall fluted minaret, linked to the Seljuk period.
  • Kesik Minaret: a building that was a Roman temple, later a Byzantine church and now a mosque, a good example of the layers of history here.
  • Clock Tower: one of the few surviving parts of the ancient city walls, with a view over the city.
  • Hıdırlık Tower: an ancient Roman tower with panoramic views of the harbour and the coast.
  • Old harbour and marina: the Roman harbour that the old town grew around; today yachts, boat trips and cafés. Mermerli Beach is just beside it if you want a swim.

Getting there

  • Walk from the city centre; the nostalgic tram stops at Kalekapısı, a convenient entrance.
  • City buses also stop at or near Kaleiçi.
  • Streets are narrow and cobbled; wear comfortable shoes.

Good to know

  • Kaleiçi is a protected historic district, so many buildings are preserved Ottoman-era houses turned into boutique hotels, guesthouses, cafés and shops.
  • Evenings are lively and pleasant in summer; mornings are better for photos and for avoiding heat.
  • Check museum opening hours and prices with official sources before you go.

Frequently asked questions

How long do you need in Kaleiçi?

A relaxed walk takes half a day; add time for lunch and the harbour.

Is Kaleiçi worth staying in?

Many travellers like its boutique hotels and atmosphere, but large beach hotels are in Konyaaltı and Lara.
