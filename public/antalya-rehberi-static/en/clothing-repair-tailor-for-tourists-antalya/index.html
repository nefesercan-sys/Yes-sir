Clothing Repair and Tailor for Tourists in Antalya

A broken zip or a dress that needs adjusting should not spoil your holiday.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Common holiday repairs

  • Trouser and jeans hemming (from ₺200).
  • Zipper replacement (from ₺300).
  • Evening dress and wedding dress alterations (from ₺800).
  • Waist and size adjustments, sleeve shortening, button and seam repair.
  • Made-to-measure sewing.

How it works

  • Send a photo of the item on WhatsApp for a price and time quote: +90 531 898 64 18.
  • Many repairs such as hems and zips are often done the same day; dresses and jackets usually the next day.
  • Open every day 08:00–23:00. Languages: Turkish, English, Russian and German.
  • Hotel and home pick-up and delivery is free in Konyaaltı; for other areas the fee depends on distance and is agreed on WhatsApp.

Where

The workshop is Terzi Can in Hurma, Konyaaltı, Antalya. More details and prices: swaphubs.com tailor service.

Ask us about this

Leave your request and we will reply by email or phone. We do not sell tours ourselves; we answer questions and can point you to licensed providers.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a tailor near Konyaaltı beach?

Yes, Terzi Can in Konyaaltı works daily from 08:00 to 23:00.

Can you collect clothes from my hotel?

Pick-up and delivery is free in Konyaaltı; elsewhere the fee depends on distance. Write on WhatsApp.
