Antalya with Kids

A shortlist of family-friendly places and how to plan around the heat.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Indoor and water attractions

  • Antalya Aquarium: has one of the world's longest underwater tunnels, with sharks and rays; a good escape from midday heat.
  • Land of Legends: described as Turkey's largest theme park, with an aqua section of wave pools and slides.
  • Water parks: many hotels and parks have water slides and wave pools; check age and height rules.

Educational and outdoor ideas

  • Dinopark: about a 40-minute drive from Antalya, with fossil pools and dinosaur exhibits.
  • Sandland: sand sculptures, especially atmospheric around sunset.
  • MiniCity Antalya: miniatures of famous Turkish landmarks, can be walked in under an hour.
  • Antalya Toy Museum: toys from different eras.
  • Karaalioğlu Park: large city park with playgrounds, picnic areas and cliff views.
  • Tünektepe Cable Car: rises to 605 m for coastline views; there is a café at the top.

Planning with children

  • Plan indoor attractions for midday and outdoor ones for morning or evening.
  • Bring water, sun hats and high-factor sunscreen.
  • Choose a sand beach such as Lara if children dislike pebbles, or bring water shoes for Konyaaltı.
  • Check opening hours, age limits and prices with official sources before you go.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best rainy or hot-day activity for kids in Antalya?

The Antalya Aquarium is a popular indoor option.

Is Antalya good for small children?

Yes, with sun protection and a plan for midday heat; Lara's sand beach is a favourite for families.
