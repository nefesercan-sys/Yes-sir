Köprülü Canyon Rafting

Rafting in Köprülü Canyon is one of the best-known adventure days from Antalya and Belek.

Updated: 2026-10-24

What it is

  • White-water rafting on grade 3–4 rapids, described as suitable for beginners.
  • The course is around 25 km, mostly between Kasımlar and Değirmenözü, with a half-day tour being common.
  • Operators also offer ziplining and canyoning in the same area.
  • The canyon is roughly an hour inland from Belek.

What to bring

  • Swimwear, a towel and dry change of clothes.
  • Water shoes or secure sandals.
  • Waterproof bag for phone and keys; sun cream.

Good to know

  • Water levels vary by season, which changes how intense the rapids feel.
  • Age and swimming-ability rules apply; follow the safety briefing and wear the life jacket and helmet.
  • Confirm what is included: transfer, lunch, photos and insurance.

Frequently asked questions

Is Köprülü Canyon rafting suitable for beginners?

Tour platforms describe the grade 3–4 rapids as suitable for beginners, with guides and a safety briefing.

How long does a rafting tour take?

A half-day tour is common, plus the transfer time.
