Common Tourist Mistakes in Antalya

Small things that cost time, money or comfort, and the simple fix for each.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Plan and book

  • Choosing the wrong area: a cheap hotel far from the airport and sights can cost hours in transfers. Check distances first.
  • Unclear transfer terms: ask whether the price is per vehicle or per person, and whether flight tracking and child seats are included.
  • Late arrival without a plan: pre-book a transfer or know your taxi route.

Heat, sun and health

  • Sightseeing at midday in summer: visit ancient sites in the morning.
  • Underestimating the sun: use high-factor sunscreen, a hat and drink water.
  • Drinking tap water: bottled water is generally recommended.
  • No travel insurance: check that it covers medical care.

Money and rules

  • Not agreeing a price first: ask the price of a service or taxi before you start.
  • Exchanging money at the least favourable rate: compare banks and city exchange offices with the airport.
  • Wrong dress at mosques: cover shoulders and knees and remove shoes.
  • Not checking documents: confirm passport validity and entry rules from official sources before travel.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest mistake tourists make in Antalya?

Being outside in the midday summer sun without protection or water.

Should I agree the taxi price first?

Taxis use a meter; confirm the destination and ask for the meter, or pre-book a transfer with a fixed price.
