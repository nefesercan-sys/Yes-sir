Jeep, Quad and Buggy Safari in Antalya

Off-road tours in the Taurus Mountains combine forest tracks, rivers and waterfalls. A practical overview.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Options

  • Jeep safari (full day): open-top 4×4 on forest tracks through the Taurus Mountains, with waterfalls, river swimming and a village lunch.
  • Quad bike tour (about half a day): riding on mountain trails, usually combined with a waterfall or river swim.
  • Buggy safari: a similar off-road experience in a small two-seat vehicle, offered alongside quad and rafting tours.

Who it suits

  • Groups and families who want an active day without hiking.
  • Quad and buggy tours usually have age, driving-licence and weight requirements; check with the operator.
  • Not ideal if you suffer from back problems or travel sickness.

Safety and tips

  • Wear closed shoes and clothes you do not mind getting dusty or wet; bring a swimsuit and towel.
  • Helmets and briefing should be provided; follow the guide.
  • Sun cream and water are essential.
  • Confirm insurance coverage with the operator and your travel insurance.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a jeep safari?

Typically a full day, with hotel pick-up and a village lunch.

Do I need a driving licence for a quad?

Many operators require one, and age limits apply; ask the operator before you book.
