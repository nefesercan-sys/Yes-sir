Day Trips from Antalya: Alanya, Side, Kemer, Kaş

Beyond the resorts, these destinations are the most booked day trips from Antalya.

Updated: 2026-10-24

The classic choices

  • Side: a coastal town with a Roman theatre and ruins in its old town; about 70 km from the airport.
  • Alanya: a separate resort town far to the east, about 130 km and 90–120 minutes from the airport, so a long day.
  • Kemer: pine forest, beaches, nearby Phaselis and the Olympos cable car to Tahtalı; about 45 km from the airport.
  • Kaş and Kekova: a boat cruise from Kaş to the Kekova area is a popular longer excursion.
  • Pamukkale: about 3 hours each way; a very long but popular day.

How to choose

  • Short on time: Side and Kemer are the easiest.
  • Love boats and quiet bays: a Kaş and Kekova cruise.
  • Prefer fewer travel hours: stay in a resort close to the sights you want.
  • Compare organised tours, a rental car and private driver; check the operator licence.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best day trip from Antalya?

Popular choices are Side, Kemer, Alanya, Kaş and Pamukkale; Side and Kemer are the easiest.

Is Alanya a good day trip?

It is about 130 km from the airport, so it is a long day; many travellers prefer to stay there.
