Pamukkale Day Trip from Antalya

Pamukkale is a long day trip but one of the most memorable. Here is how to plan it.

Updated: 2026-10-24

What you see

  • White terraces: calcium-carbonate terraces that look like frozen cotton.
  • Thermal pools: warm mineral pools where you can usually bathe.
  • Hierapolis: the ancient Roman city above the terraces, with theatre and ruins.

Time and logistics

  • It is about 3 hours by road from Antalya, so a day trip lasts roughly 14 hours including stops.
  • Tours normally include hotel pick-up; start is very early.
  • You can also stay overnight in the Pamukkale area to avoid the long day.

Tips

  • Take swimwear and a towel for the thermal pools.
  • Rules for walking on the terraces (for example barefoot) can change; follow the signs and the guide.
  • Wear sun protection; there is little shade on the terraces.
  • Check current opening hours and entry rules with official sources.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Pamukkale from Antalya?

About 3 hours by road each way.

Is a Pamukkale day trip too long?

It is a long day of about 14 hours; consider staying overnight if you prefer a slower pace.
