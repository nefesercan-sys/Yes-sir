Belek Guide

Belek is a purpose-built resort area known for golf and large all-inclusive hotels, about 25–35 minutes from the airport.

Updated: 2026-10-24

What Belek is known for

  • Golf: well over a dozen courses, including Montgomerie, Carya (floodlit, with night golf), Sueno, Cornelia and Kaya Palazzo.
  • Beach: the public beach has been awarded the Blue Flag and has roughly 200 m of golden sand; many resorts also have their own beach sections.
  • Resorts: large all-inclusive hotels, popular with families and golfers.

Day trips from Belek

  • Aspendos: the Roman theatre is not far from the coast and often hosts opera and ballet performances.
  • Köprülü Canyon: about an hour north, popular for rafting, zip-lining and canyoning.
  • Evening shows: the Gloria Aspendos Arena hosts the Fire of Anatolia show, usually from April to November.

Is Belek right for you?

  • Yes: golf trips, families who want an all-inclusive resort, and a quiet resort environment.
  • Less so: if you want a lively city, walkable shops and cafés, or independent evenings out; Konyaaltı or Kaleiçi suit that better.
  • Season: golf is busiest from October to April; summer is hot for a round.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Belek from Antalya Airport?

About 35 km, roughly 25–35 minutes by car.

Is Belek good for golf in winter?

Yes. Courses are busiest from October to April.
