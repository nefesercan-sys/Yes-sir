Day Trips from Antalya

Antalya is surrounded by Roman cities, canyons and mountains. A practical shortlist.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Ancient sites

  • Aspendos: one of the best-preserved Roman theatres, still used for performances; about 45 minutes from the city.
  • Perge: a large Roman-era city with colonnaded streets and a stadium; easy to combine with Aspendos in one day.
  • Termessos: a dramatically sited ancient city in the Taurus Mountains; wear sturdy shoes.
  • Side: a Roman theatre and ruins in its old town on the coast.

In and near the city

  • Kaleiçi: the old town with Ottoman houses and a harbour in use for over two thousand years, plus Hadrian's Gate at its edge.
  • Düden Waterfalls: upper and lower cascades; the lower falls drop into the Mediterranean. Each needs only a couple of hours.

Nature and adventure

  • Köprülü Canyon: a popular white-water rafting spot, roughly an hour inland.
  • Olympos and Çıralı: a quieter, nature-focused area west of the resorts, known for the Chimaera flames.

Planning tips

  • Visit ancient sites in the morning in summer to avoid the heat.
  • Take water, a hat and good shoes.
  • Opening hours and entry fees change; confirm them with official sources.
  • A rental car, organised tour or private driver all work; compare before booking.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best day trip from Antalya?

Aspendos combined with Perge is the classic choice: two major Roman sites in one day.

Is Termessos difficult?

It is in the mountains with uneven paths, so wear sturdy shoes and bring water.
