Kemer Guide

Kemer combines sea, pine forest and mountains. A short guide to what to see and who it suits.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Highlights

  • Kemer Beach: the closest beach to town, pebbly, with a forested Tahtalı backdrop.
  • Moonlight Beach and Yörük Park: beyond the marina; the park is an open-air folklore museum.
  • Phaselis: a beautiful, quiet bay about a 15-minute drive south of Kemer, with Greco-Roman ruins of an ancient seaport.
  • Olympos Cable Car (Tahtalı): rises to a summit at 2,365 m with views over the coast and mountains; the base is at Tekirova, roughly half an hour southwest of Kemer.
  • Alacasu (Paradise Cove): a cove about 20 minutes south by car.

Who Kemer suits

  • Travellers who like nature, forest and mountain scenery alongside the sea.
  • Couples and families looking for a resort strip with a marina.
  • Those happy with a 40–55 minute transfer from the airport (about 45 km).

Tips

  • Take water and sun protection for Phaselis and the mountain trips.
  • Weather at altitude is cooler; bring a light layer for the cable car.
  • Check cable car hours and weather conditions on the day.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Kemer from Antalya Airport?

About 45 km, usually 40–55 minutes.

Is Kemer beach sand?

Kemer Beach is pebbly; check each resort's beach type before booking.
