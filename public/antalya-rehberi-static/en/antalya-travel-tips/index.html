Antalya Travel Tips

The small practical details that make a holiday smoother.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Money

  • Currency is the Turkish lira (TRY). Cards are widely accepted in hotels and restaurants; keep some cash for small shops, markets and tips.
  • Tipping of around 10% in restaurants is customary but not compulsory.
  • Compare exchange rates; airport counters are usually less favourable than banks or city exchange offices.

Electricity, phone and internet

  • Sockets are European type C/F, 230 V. UK and US visitors need an adapter.
  • Check roaming costs with your provider. An eSIM or a local SIM can be cheaper for data.
  • Free Wi-Fi is common in hotels and cafés.

Health and safety

  • The emergency number is 112.
  • Tap water is generally not recommended for drinking; use bottled water.
  • The sun is strong; use sunscreen and drink plenty of water.
  • Pharmacies (eczane) are common and can advise on minor ailments; duty pharmacies are open at night.
  • Check your travel insurance covers medical care.

Etiquette

  • Cover shoulders and knees in mosques and remove shoes.
  • Ask before photographing people.
  • Entry and visa rules depend on your passport; check the official foreign ministry or Turkish government sources before you travel.

Frequently asked questions

What is the emergency number in Turkey?

112.

Can I drink tap water in Antalya?

It is generally recommended to drink bottled water.

What plug do I need?

European type C or F sockets at 230 V.
