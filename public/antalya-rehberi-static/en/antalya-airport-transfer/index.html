Antalya Airport (AYT) Transfer Guide

Distances and typical drive times from the airport to the main holiday areas, and how to choose between a private transfer, a taxi and public transport.

Your options

  • Pre-booked private transfer: a fixed price agreed in advance, the driver tracks your flight and waits if it is delayed. The most comfortable choice for families and late arrivals.
  • Taxi: metered, so the fare depends on traffic and route. Agree on the destination clearly and ask for the meter.
  • Public transport: the cheapest option for Antalya city and some areas, but stops can be far from your hotel and it is awkward with large luggage. Check current routes before you rely on it.
  • Hotel shuttle: many resorts offer one; ask in advance whether it is included or paid.

Tips for a smooth arrival

  • Send your flight number to the transfer company so they can track delays.
  • Ask whether the price is per vehicle or per person and whether child seats are available.
  • Keep the hotel name and address on your phone, ideally in Turkish script too.
  • Have a little Turkish lira or a working card for small expenses on arrival.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Antalya Airport from the city?

About 12 km to the centre, usually 10–20 minutes depending on traffic.

How long is the transfer from Antalya Airport to Konyaaltı?

Roughly 20–25 km and 25–40 minutes, because the route goes through the city.

Is a private transfer better than a taxi?

A private transfer gives a fixed price and flight tracking; a taxi is metered. For a late arrival or a family with luggage, a pre-booked transfer is simpler.
