What to Eat in Antalya

Beyond the hotel buffet: a short list of local dishes.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Antalya specialities

  • Piyaz: a white bean salad with a tahini-based dressing, a signature of the region, often eaten as a starter or alongside meat.
  • Tandır kebab: slow-cooked lamb or beef, tender and served in many local restaurants.
  • Şiş köfte: grilled minced-meat skewers.
  • Hibeş: a tahini-based spread typical of Antalya, eaten with bread.

Everyday Turkish food

  • Turkish breakfast: cheese, olives, tomatoes, cucumber, eggs, jams, bread and tea.
  • Meze: small cold dishes to share.
  • Fresh fish and seafood near the harbour and along the coast.
  • Turkish tea (çay): offered everywhere, served in small glasses.

Tips

  • Ask if a dish is spicy; many are mild.
  • Vegetarians do well with meze, salads and vegetable dishes; ask about meat stock.
  • Check whether a service charge is already included before tipping.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical dish of Antalya?

Piyaz, a white bean salad with a tahini-based dressing, is closely associated with the city.

Is Antalya food spicy?

Mostly mild; ask if you want it spicier or milder.
