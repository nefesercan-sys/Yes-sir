Boat Trips in Antalya

A boat day is one of the most booked activities on the coast. Here is what the main types include and what to check.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Main types

  • Full-day bay cruise (about 6–8 hours): a coastal cruise to bays with swimming and snorkelling stops, usually a hot lunch on board and often hotel pick-up.
  • Sunset cruise (about 2–3 hours): a late-afternoon coastal trip with drinks on board.
  • Island and canyon trips: offers such as a Suluada island trip or a boat trip in the Green Canyon are popular options listed by tour platforms.

What to check before booking

  • What is included: lunch, drinks, snorkelling gear, hotel transfer.
  • Pick-up time and total duration, including the transfer.
  • Age and swimming rules for children.
  • That the operator is licensed; verify travel agencies and facilities on the official sites of the Ministry of Culture and Tourism and TÜRSAB.
  • Cancellation and weather policy.

What to bring

  • Swimwear, towel, sun cream, hat and sunglasses.
  • Cash for extras such as drinks or photos.
  • A light layer for the evening and motion-sickness tablets if you are sensitive.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a boat trip in Antalya?

A full-day cruise is typically 6–8 hours; a sunset cruise about 2–3 hours.

Is lunch included on boat trips?

Full-day cruises commonly include a hot lunch on board; always confirm what is included before booking.
