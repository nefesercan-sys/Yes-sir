Antalya in Winter

Winter is not a swimming season in Antalya, but it is a very good one for sightseeing, golf and low prices.

Updated: 2026-10-24

What to expect

  • Air temperatures are typically around 9–17 °C and the sea around 16–18 °C: too cool for most swimmers.
  • Rain is more likely than in summer, so bring a rain jacket and a warm layer for evenings.
  • Hotel rates are lowest from November to March, and beaches and attractions are quiet.

What to do

  • Sightseeing: Kaleiçi, Aspendos, Perge and Termessos are comfortable in cool weather.
  • Golf: Belek courses are busiest from October to April.
  • Skiing: Saklıkent ski centre is about an hour from the city; the season usually runs from late December to April depending on the weather, and the chairlift reaches 2,500 m.
  • Relax: Turkish bath (hamam) and spa days are a good choice on rainy days.

Is it worth it?

Yes, if you want culture, golf, quiet and good value. If you want a beach and swimming, choose late May to early October.

Frequently asked questions

Can you swim in Antalya in winter?

The sea is about 16–18 °C, which is cold for most people.

Is there skiing near Antalya?

Yes, Saklıkent is about an hour from the city, usually open from late December to April depending on the weather.
