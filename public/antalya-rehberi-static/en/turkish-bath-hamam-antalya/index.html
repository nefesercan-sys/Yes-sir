Turkish Bath (Hamam) in Antalya

A hamam is a classic Turkish experience. Here is what happens and how to choose.

Updated: 2026-10-24

What happens

  • A hamam session usually lasts about 1.5–2 hours.
  • Typical packages include heat, a full scrub and a foam massage; oil massage and sauna are often optional extras.
  • Some hamams offer packages of about 90 minutes with hotel pick-up.

Etiquette

  • You are given a cloth (peştemal) or you wear swimwear; ask what is expected.
  • Men and women are usually in separate sections or at separate times, unless it is a tourist hamam.
  • Avoid a heavy meal before; drink water.

Check before booking

  • What is included and the total price, including tips and extras.
  • Hygiene and reviews.
  • Health: if you are pregnant, have heart or blood-pressure problems or are unwell, ask a doctor first.
