Cappadocia Tour from Antalya

Cappadocia is far from Antalya, so most visitors go on a 2–3 day tour, often by flight.

Updated: 2026-10-24

What you see

  • Fairy chimneys, valleys and rock-cut churches.
  • Underground cities.
  • Hot-air balloon rides at sunrise (an optional add-on).

How tours work

  • Tours usually last 2–3 days.
  • Most include return flights and one or two nights of accommodation.
  • Balloon flights depend on weather; ask about the cancellation or refund policy.

Tips

  • Dress warmly for dawn balloon flights, even in summer.
  • Check what is included: flights, hotel, transfers, meals and entry fees.
  • Book balloon rides early in high season.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do you need for Cappadocia from Antalya?

Most tours are 2–3 days.

Are balloon rides included?

Often they are an optional extra; confirm before booking, and note they depend on weather.
