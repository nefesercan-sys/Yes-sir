Where to Stay in Antalya

The area you choose shapes your holiday more than the hotel. A quick comparison of the main bases.

Updated: 2026-10-24

How to choose

  • First time, want city and sights: Kaleiçi, Konyaaltı or Lara.
  • Beach and all-inclusive with children: Lara, Belek or Side.
  • Golf: Belek.
  • Nature and mountains: Kemer area.
  • Long stay with a local feel: Konyaaltı offers shops, cafés and services within walking distance.

Practical thought

Transfer time adds up: Alanya is about 90–120 minutes from the airport, Konyaaltı 25–40 minutes. If you want to explore a lot, a central base saves hours.

Frequently asked questions

Which area of Antalya is best for a first visit?

Kaleiçi, Konyaaltı and Lara are the easiest first bases: close to the airport, sights and services.

Where is the best area for families?

Lara, Belek and Side have large family resorts; Konyaaltı is good if you prefer a city setting with a long beach.
