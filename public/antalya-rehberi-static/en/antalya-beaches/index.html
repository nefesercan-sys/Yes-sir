Beaches in Antalya: Konyaaltı vs Lara

The two big city beaches are very different. Here is how to choose.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Konyaaltı

Konyaaltı is about 13 km long, mainly fine pebbles with some sand, backed by the Taurus Mountains, and it holds Blue Flag status for water quality. It is on the western side of the city and developed, with beach clubs, promenade and cafés. It is busy in summer, but its length means you can usually find space.

Lara

Lara lies east of the centre and is a sand beach, popular with families who want soft sand underfoot. It is also developed and lined with beach clubs, and many large hotels are located here.

Which one for you?

  • Sand preference, small children: Lara.
  • Long walks, mountain views, a lively city atmosphere: Konyaaltı.
  • Pebbles: bring water shoes, they make entering the sea much easier.

Beach tips

  • Sun is strong from late spring to autumn; reapply sunscreen often.
  • Beach clubs charge for sunbeds and umbrellas; public sections are free but have fewer facilities.
  • Take drinking water and avoid midday sun, especially with children.
  • Check flag and lifeguard signals before swimming.

Frequently asked questions

Is Konyaaltı beach sand or pebbles?

Mainly fine pebbles with some sand.

Is Lara a sand beach?

Yes, Lara is a sand beach east of the city centre.

Which Antalya beach is better for families?

Many families prefer Lara for its sand, while Konyaaltı suits those who like long promenade walks; both are developed with facilities.
