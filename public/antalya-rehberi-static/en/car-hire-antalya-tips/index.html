Car Hire in Antalya: Tips

A rental car gives freedom for day trips, but a few checks save money and stress.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Before you rent

  • Check which driving licence you need and whether an international permit is required, from official sources and the rental company.
  • Compare insurance options and what excess you pay in case of damage.
  • Ask about the deposit, usually held on a credit card.
  • Read the fuel policy; full-to-full is the simplest.

At pick-up

  • Photograph all existing damage and have it noted on the contract.
  • Check tyres, lights and air conditioning.
  • Ask how motorway tolls are charged and whether the car has a toll tag.

On the road

  • Traffic drives on the right; follow speed limits and watch for speed cameras.
  • Parking in busy areas can be hard; ask your hotel.
  • If you only want airport and beach trips, a transfer or taxi is often simpler than a rental.

Frequently asked questions

Is it easy to drive in Antalya?

Main roads are good, but city traffic and parking can be busy in summer.

Do I need a credit card to rent a car?

Most companies hold a deposit on a credit card; confirm with the company.
