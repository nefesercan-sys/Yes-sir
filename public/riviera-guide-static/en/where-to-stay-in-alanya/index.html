Where to Stay in Alanya

Alanya stretches along the coast, so the district you pick matters.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Quick comparison

AreaCharacter
Centre and harbourCastle, harbour, restaurants, most sights within reach
Cleopatra BeachAbout 2 km of sand beach close to the centre
MahmutlarEast of the centre, more residential feel
KonaklıWest of the centre, resort hotels

How to choose

  • Sights and evenings: the centre.
  • Beach: near Cleopatra Beach.
  • Quiet or longer stays: Mahmutlar or the outskirts.
  • Check distance and transport to the beach and centre before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Which part of Alanya is best for first-timers?

The centre and the area near Cleopatra Beach put sights, beach and restaurants close together.

Is Alanya spread out?

Yes, it stretches along the coast, so check distances.
