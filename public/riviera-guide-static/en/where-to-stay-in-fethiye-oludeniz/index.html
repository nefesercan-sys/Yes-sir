Where to Stay in Fethiye and Ölüdeniz

Fethiye is a working town with a harbour; Ölüdeniz is the beach resort.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Quick comparison

AreaCharacter
FethiyeHarbour town with Lycian rock tombs, markets, boats
ÖlüdenizBeach resort and Blue Lagoon; start of paragliding flights
GöcekQuiet yachting town about 22 km from the airport
KayaköyAbandoned village about 15 minutes from Fethiye

How to choose

  • Beach and activities: Ölüdeniz.
  • Town life and boats: Fethiye.
  • Quiet: Göcek.
  • Plan transport: day trips are easier by dolmuş or tour from Fethiye.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ölüdeniz in Fethiye?

Ölüdeniz is a beach resort in the Fethiye district, about 65 km from Dalaman airport.

Which is better for a short stay?

Ölüdeniz for beach and paragliding; Fethiye for a town base and day trips.
