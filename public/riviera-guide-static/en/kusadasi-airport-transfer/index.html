Getting to Kuşadası

Kuşadası is reached through Izmir Adnan Menderes Airport.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Distances from Izmir Airport (ADB)

PlaceDistanceDrive time
Kuşadası≈95 km75–90 min
Selçuk≈68 km50–60 min
Ephesus≈70 km55–65 min

Other ways

  • The regional train from Izmir Basmane to Selçuk takes about 80 minutes.
  • A seasonal ferry runs between Samos (Greece) and Kuşadası; a passport is required.
  • Kuşadası is a busy cruise port; allow extra time on ship days.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Kuşadası from Izmir airport?

About 95 km, roughly 75–90 minutes.

Is there a ferry to Samos?

Yes, a seasonal ferry; check the schedule and bring your passport.
