Things to Do in Kaş

Small, scenic and active.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Highlights

  • Diving: sunken aircraft, shipwrecks and underwater caves.
  • Meis (Kastellorizo): a Greek island about 1,800 m offshore; the boat ride takes about 20 minutes; bring your passport.
  • King's Tomb: a Lycian tomb carved into the rock, the symbol of the town.
  • Lycian Way: a long hiking route with Kaş as one of its stage points.

Tips

  • Check entry rules for Greece before going to Meis.
  • Ask diving operators about licences and safety.
