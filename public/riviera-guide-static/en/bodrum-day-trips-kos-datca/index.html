Day Trips from Bodrum

Bodrum is a good base for boats, islands and the peninsula itself.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Options

  • Kos (Greece): the ferry takes about 20 minutes according to a travel guide; sailings can be cancelled in bad weather. Bring your passport and check Greek entry rules first.
  • Datça: ferries run from Bodrum towards the Reşadiye peninsula near Datça, where buses continue to Marmaris.
  • Boat days: popular around the peninsula with swim stops; check what is included.

Before you book

  • Check passport and visa rules for the island you plan to visit.
  • Ask about cancellation if the weather is poor.
  • Check that the operator is licensed on official sites.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the ferry from Bodrum to Kos?

About 20 minutes according to a travel guide; check the current timetable.

Do I need a passport for Kos?

Yes, you need valid travel documents; check the current entry rules before you go.
