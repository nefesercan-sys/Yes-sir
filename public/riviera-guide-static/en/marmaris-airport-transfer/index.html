Getting to Marmaris

Marmaris is served by Dalaman Airport. Here is the route and the main choices.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Distance and time

  • Marmaris is roughly 90–100 km from Dalaman Airport (sources give slightly different figures).
  • A private transfer or taxi usually takes about 1.5 hours; shared shuttles take longer because of several stops.
  • The route follows the D400 highway, with a pine-forest mountain pass and views over Marmaris Bay.
  • İçmeler is a little further than Marmaris centre, and Turunç further still.

Options

  • Private transfer or taxi: door to door.
  • Bus: drops you at the Marmaris bus station, not at hotels.
  • Shared shuttle: cheaper but slower; ask about the number of stops.

Tips

  • Tell the provider your exact hotel area: İçmeler, Turunç and Armutalan are all different.
  • Agree the pick-up point and flight tracking in advance.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Marmaris from Dalaman airport?

About 90–100 km, roughly 1.5 hours by private transfer.

Is Marmaris close to Bodrum airport?

No; Dalaman is the usual airport for Marmaris.
