Day Trips from Marmaris

Boats, rivers and ancient sites within reach of Marmaris.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Classics

  • Dalyan: about 1–1.5 hours from Marmaris; riverboat trips through the delta, mud baths, rock tombs, Kaunos and Iztuzu Beach, a loggerhead turtle nesting site.
  • Rhodes (Greece): a day tour of around 8 hours by catamaran; bring a valid passport or ID as required and check entry rules.
  • Datça and Knidos: a scenic peninsula with villages and the ancient ruins of Knidos.
  • Bozburun peninsula: traditional villages such as Söğüt, Selimiye and Orhaniye, fish restaurants and calm bays.
  • Fethiye and Ölüdeniz: about 1.5–2 hours east.
  • Pamukkale: about 3–4 hours inland; an organised trip takes roughly 12–14 hours.

Tips

  • Turtle nesting areas have rules; follow signs and your guide.
  • Combine nearby places rather than far ones if time is short.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dalyan a good day trip from Marmaris?

Yes: about 1–1.5 hours away with river trips, mud baths and Iztuzu Beach.

How long is the Rhodes day trip?

Around 8 hours in total; check the operator and bring your passport.
