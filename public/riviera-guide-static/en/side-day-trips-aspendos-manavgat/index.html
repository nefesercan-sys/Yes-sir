Day Trips from Side

Ancient sites, river trips and canyon adventures are all in reach.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Options

  • Aspendos: one of the best-preserved Roman theatres, still used for performances.
  • Manavgat: river boat trips and the waterfall.
  • Köprülü Canyon: rafting on grade 3–4 rapids, described as suitable for beginners.
  • Antalya old town: about 70 km away.

Before you book

  • Ask what is included: transfer, lunch, tickets.
  • Check the weather and water level for rafting.
  • Compare organised tours with a private driver.

Frequently asked questions

Is Aspendos near Side?

Yes, it is within a day trip; check the current drive time.
