Things to Do in Marmaris

A lively resort town built around a big natural bay.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Highlights

  • Marina and bay: the centre of town and the start of many boat days and gulet cruises.
  • Bar Street: the best-known nightlife area.
  • Boat trips: to bays south of town and to Cleopatra (Sedir) Island, known for its pale sand.
  • Active days: diving, kayaking and rafting are offered by operators in the region.

Tips

  • Ask what a boat day includes: lunch, drinks, snorkelling gear.
  • Pick operators with a licence; check on official sites.
  • Summers are hot; bring sun protection.

Frequently asked questions

What is Marmaris known for?

Its bay and marina, boat cruises and nightlife, especially Bar Street.

What is Cleopatra Island known for?

A pale sand beach reached by boat.
