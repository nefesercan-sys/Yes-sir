Things to Do in Bodrum

A short list of the sights that make most visitors' plans.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Sights

  • Bodrum Castle (Castle of St Peter): built by the Knights of St John in the 15th century, overlooking the harbour.
  • Museum of Underwater Archaeology: inside the castle, with shipwreck finds from the ancient world.
  • Mausoleum of Halicarnassus: a 4th-century BC tomb that was one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World; today only ruins remain.
  • Ancient Theatre of Halicarnassus: 4th century BC, once seating over 13,000 people, used for summer events.

Beaches and evenings

  • Beach clubs and water sports at Gümbet; calmer swimming at Yahşi and Aspat.
  • Harbour and marina restaurants in the evening.

Practical

  • Check opening hours and ticket rules on official sources; they change by season.
  • Wear comfortable shoes: the old streets and ruins are uneven.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main sight in Bodrum?

Bodrum Castle (Castle of St Peter), which also houses the Museum of Underwater Archaeology.

Can I see the Mausoleum?

Only ruins remain of the Mausoleum of Halicarnassus.
