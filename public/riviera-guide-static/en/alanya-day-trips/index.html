Day Trips from Alanya

Alanya works as a base for boats, ruins and the coast.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Options

  • Boat trip: day cruises along the coast with swim stops; check what is included.
  • Syedra: ancient ruins about 20 km from the centre.
  • Side: Roman theatre and Temple of Apollo to the west.
  • Antalya: about 130 km away, a long day trip.

Before you book

  • Check the distance: Alanya is far from Antalya and Side.
  • Ask about pick-up from your hotel.
  • Check operators for a licence on official sites.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit Antalya from Alanya in a day?

Yes, but it is about 130 km each way, so it is a long day.

Is Side close to Alanya?

Side is west of Alanya; check the current drive time.
