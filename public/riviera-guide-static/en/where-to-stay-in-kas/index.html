Where to Stay in Kaş

The Kaş coast is about 90 km long with several different bases.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Quick comparison

AreaCharacter
KaşHarbour town, diving, King's Tomb, boat trips
KalkanAbout 29 km west; small resort village
ÜçağızAt the east end of the coast, near Kekova

How to choose

  • Diving and town life: Kaş.
  • Quieter evenings: Kalkan or Üçağız.
  • Check how you will get to the beach from your hotel.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kaş good for diving?

It is described as Turkey's diving capital, with wrecks and underwater caves.

How far is Kalkan?

About 29 km, roughly 35 minutes.
