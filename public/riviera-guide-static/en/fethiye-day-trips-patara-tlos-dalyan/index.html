Day Trips from Fethiye

Several ancient sites and natural areas are an easy drive away.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Options

  • Tlos: rock tombs and ruins, about 35 minutes by road.
  • Saklıkent Gorge: about 45 minutes; often combined with Tlos.
  • Patara Beach: about 1 hour; allow at least 4 hours. Turtle nesting season runs from May to October.
  • Dalyan: river trips and Iztuzu Beach; farther west.
  • Pamukkale: about 4 hours from Fethiye, a very long day; consider an overnight stay.
  • Lycian Way: a walking route of about 500 km from Fethiye towards Antalya; short sections work for day hikes.

Tips

  • Suggested stay: 2–4 full days to see the main places.
  • Combine Tlos and Saklıkent to save time.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine Tlos and Saklıkent?

Yes, they are in the same direction and are often combined.

Is Pamukkale a good day trip from Fethiye?

It takes about 4 hours each way; an overnight stay is easier.
