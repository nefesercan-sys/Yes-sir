Where to Stay in Marmaris

Marmaris sits on a large bay surrounded by pine hills, with several different bases.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Quick comparison

AreaCharacter
Marmaris centreMarina, bar street, nightlife, starting point for gulet cruises
İçmelerFamily-friendly beach town; dolmuş from Marmaris about 15–20 minutes
TurunçSmall quiet town among pine forests; dolmuş about 30–40 minutes
ArmutalanBudget-friendly, local feel

How to choose

  • Nightlife and boats: the centre and marina.
  • Families: İçmeler.
  • Peace and nature: Turunç.
  • Check how you will travel to the beach and town from your hotel.
