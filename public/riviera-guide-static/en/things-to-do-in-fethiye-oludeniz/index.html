Things to Do in Fethiye and Ölüdeniz

Beach, mountain and canyon within a short drive.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Highlights

  • Ölüdeniz and the Blue Lagoon: the best-known beach area.
  • Paragliding: flights start from almost 2,000 m on Babadağ and land at sea level on the beach.
  • Babadağ cable car: stops at about 1,200 m and 1,700 m.
  • Butterfly Valley: essentially reachable only by boat.
  • Kayaköy: a village abandoned in the 1920s, about 15 minutes from Fethiye.
  • Saklıkent Gorge: 18 km long and about 300 m high, with about 4 km walkable; around 45 minutes from Fethiye.
  • Lycian rock tombs: 4th century BC, above Fethiye.

Tips

  • Paragliding depends on wind and weather; ask about cancellation.
  • For Saklıkent bring water shoes; the gorge involves cold water.
