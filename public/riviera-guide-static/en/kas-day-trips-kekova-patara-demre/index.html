Day Trips from Kaş

Boats, beaches and ancient sites around the Lycian coast.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Options

  • Kekova: boat tours from the Üçağız area.
  • Kalkan: about 29 km west.
  • Patara Beach: a long beach on the way towards Fethiye (about an hour from Fethiye); turtle season runs May to October.
  • Demre: about 45 km east.
  • Meis: day trip to the Greek island with passport.

Tips

  • Combine nearby places in a single day.
  • Check boat departures and weather.
