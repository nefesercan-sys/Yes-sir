Where to Stay in Kuşadası

One of the busiest beach resorts in Turkey.

AreaCharacter

How to choose

  • Evenings and nightlife: the centre.
  • Beach life: Ladies Beach.
  • Water sports: beaches on the Selçuk road.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kuşadası a good base for Ephesus?

Yes, Ephesus is about 19 km from the Kuşadası port, roughly 25 minutes by road.

Which beach is best?

Ladies Beach is known for powdery sand and many cafés.
