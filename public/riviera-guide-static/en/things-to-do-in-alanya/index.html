Things to Do in Alanya

A compact list of what most visitors see.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Sights

  • Alanya Castle: open daily and reached by aerial tram in addition to the walk; check current hours.
  • Red Tower: a 14th-century landmark by the harbour.
  • Cleopatra Beach: about 2 km of sand with free public access.
  • Caves: Damlataş Cave by the harbour and Dim Cave outside town, about 11 km away.
  • Syedra: an ancient city about 20 km from the centre, free to enter according to a travel guide.

Climate and events

  • Over 300 sunny days a year are claimed; July is the warmest month and January the coolest.
  • Festivals include tourism and art in June, jazz in September and cycling in October.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main sight in Alanya?

Alanya Castle above the harbour, reachable by aerial tram.

Is Cleopatra Beach free?

Public access is free; sunbeds and facilities may cost extra.
