Getting to Bodrum

Most visitors arrive at Milas-Bodrum Airport. Here is what to expect on the way to your hotel.

Updated: 2026-10-24

From Milas-Bodrum Airport (BJV)

  • Bodrum town is about 33 km from the airport, roughly 40–45 minutes by road.
  • Yalıkavak on the north-west of the peninsula is about 53 km, roughly an hour.
  • Times for other peninsula towns vary with traffic and the exact hotel; ask your hotel or transfer provider.
  • Airport shuttle buses stop at places such as the Bodrum bus station; a private transfer or taxi goes door to door.

Other ways to arrive

  • Izmir airport: far more flights, then about 250 km and roughly 3.5–4 hours by road to Bodrum.
  • Istanbul: about 830 km by road, roughly 13 hours by bus; most people fly.
  • By sea: ferries connect Bodrum with Kos and, in summer, Rhodes; sailings can be cancelled in bad weather. Check entry rules for Greece before you travel.
  • There is no train service to Bodrum.

Tips

  • Compare a private transfer, a shared shuttle and a taxi for your hotel location.
  • Within the peninsula, minibuses (dolmuş) run from the Bodrum bus station to the towns.
  • In peak summer allow extra time for traffic.
