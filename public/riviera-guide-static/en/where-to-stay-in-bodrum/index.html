Where to Stay in Bodrum

The Bodrum peninsula has very different bases. Pick by mood, not only by hotel.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Quick comparison

AreaCharacter
Bodrum town and marinaCastle, harbour, restaurants, stylish lounges with sea views
GümbetLively, beach clubs, water sports, a well-known bar street
YahşiRelaxed, soft sand, calm water, tavernas
AspatQuieter, surrounded by hills and greenery
YalıkavakNorth-west of the peninsula; about 53 km from the airport
GümüşlükSmall waterfront village known for fish restaurants

How to choose

  • Nightlife and activity: Gümbet and the marina area.
  • Culture and walks: Bodrum town, close to the castle and the museum.
  • Calm: Yahşi, Aspat and the smaller villages.
  • Remember the peninsula is large; check the distance from your hotel to the area you want to spend time in.

Frequently asked questions

Which area of Bodrum is best for nightlife?

Gümbet and the marina area are the best known for nightlife.

Which area is quiet?

Yahşi and Aspat are described as calmer, and the smaller villages are quieter still.
