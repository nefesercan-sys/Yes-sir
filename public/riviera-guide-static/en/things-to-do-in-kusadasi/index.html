Things to Do in Kuşadası

Beaches in town, a world-famous ancient city nearby.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Highlights

  • Ephesus: the Library of Celsus, the Great Theatre and the Terrace Houses; the site is about 19 km from the port.
  • House of the Virgin Mary: a nearby pilgrimage site.
  • Selçuk: the town next to Ephesus, about 20 km from Kuşadası.
  • Pigeon Island and the beaches: swimming and sunset.

Tips

  • Visit Ephesus early to avoid crowds and heat.
  • Check opening hours and tickets on official sources.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Ephesus from Kuşadası?

About 19 km, roughly 25 minutes by road.

Is Ephesus walkable?

Not from the pier; use a taxi, dolmuş or tour.
