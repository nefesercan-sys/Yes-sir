Day Trips from Kuşadası

Greece by sea, ancient sites by road.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Options

  • Samos (Greece): daily from April 1 to October 31 according to a travel guide, about 2 hours; passport required.
  • Selçuk and Ephesus: the closest and most popular.
  • Çeşme and Alaçatı: about 1.5 hours.
  • Bodrum: about 2.5 hours.
  • Pamukkale: about 3 hours; a long day.

Tips

  • Check the ferry timetable and entry rules for Greece.
  • For long days, consider staying overnight.
