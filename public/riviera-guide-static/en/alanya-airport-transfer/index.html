Getting to Alanya

Alanya has its own small airport, but many visitors arrive via Antalya.

Updated: 2026-10-24

Two airports

  • Antalya Airport (AYT): about 125–130 km to Alanya; plan roughly 1.5–2 hours by road.
  • Gazipaşa-Alanya Airport (GZP): about 41 km to the centre, with flights from some cities; check the current routes.
  • Istanbul (Sabiha Gökçen) to Alanya is a flight of about 1 hour 20 minutes.

Getting around Alanya

  • City buses and minibuses run through the day; some buses need a travel card.
  • Taxis are available; agree on the fare or check the meter.
  • Airport bus times and fares change, so check current information.

Tips

  • Ask your hotel for the exact distance from the airport: Alanya spreads along the coast.
  • A private transfer saves effort after a long flight, especially from Antalya.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Alanya from Antalya airport?

About 125–130 km, roughly 1.5–2 hours by road.

Does Alanya have an airport?

Yes, Gazipaşa-Alanya Airport is about 41 km from the centre.
